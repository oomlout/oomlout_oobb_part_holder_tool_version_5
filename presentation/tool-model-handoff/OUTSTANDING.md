# Outstanding tool evidence

The catalogue has 64 production holder layouts. Loaded assembly guides have been
generated for 53; the 11 layouts below need evidence for the actual tools before
their remaining guides can be produced. Their existing OOMP holder envelopes
describe cutouts, not measured tools, and should not be presented as tool models.

| Tool | Affected holders | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BIC clear-lid marker | One-place and six-place | Product or variant identification, capped length, barrel and cap diameters, and clip projection; a scaled side photograph would also establish its silhouette. |
| Large German marker | One-place and four-place | Brand or product identification, scaled side and end views, capped length, and maximum cap and barrel dimensions. |
| IKEA MÅLA marker | One-place and five-place | Individual capped pen length, cap and barrel diameters, and a straight-on scaled photograph. [IKEA article 201.840.41](https://www.ikea.gr/en/products/mala-felt-tip-pen-12-pack/20184041/) establishes its appearance but gives dimensions for the 12-pack, not the pen. |
| Thick patterned marker | One-place and six-place | Product identification or a scaled side and end view, capped length, cap and barrel dimensions, and pattern details. |
| TDPB M3 drill cleaner | One-place | Identity and a scaled photograph or drawing of the working end, shank and grip, plus their dimensions and overall length. The Ø3.6 × 100 mm pocket is only a clearance profile. |
| TDPB M6 drill cleaner | One-place | Identity and independent measurements and photographs of the working end, shank, grip and overall length. The Ø6.6 × 100 mm pocket is only a clearance profile. |
| TDPB nozzle changer | One-place | Identity and a scaled view or drawing showing its socket or nose, shank and grip, plus their dimensions and overall length. The Ø12 × 100 mm pocket is only a clearance profile. |

The generic 17 mm utility knife record also lacks an independently identified
specimen. Its single-pocket holder already has a loaded guide using the
photographed closed utility knife, an alternative listed for that holder. A
separate view for the generic record requires a labelled specimen or a decision
to use the photographed knife as its representative. Both alternatives occupy
the same pocket; they are not simultaneous assembly parts.

Each [tool packet](README.md) identifies its holders, quantities and staged
references. Once measurements are available, build the tool in OOMP, check the
individual and repeated placements, regenerate the holder guides, then run the
handoff staging script to refresh the queue.
