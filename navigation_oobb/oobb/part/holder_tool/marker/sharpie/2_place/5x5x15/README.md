## Assembly

Print two copies of the holder STL. The dark teal half sits against the wall and the light teal half faces the room.

1. Orient the rear half as it sits on the wall, with the pocket facing you and the opening at the top. [View step 1](../../../../../../../../parts/oobb_part_holder_tool_marker_sharpie_2_place_5x5x15/assembly/render/raw/step_01.png)
2. Face the front half toward the rear pocket. Align the mounting holes and both top openings. [View step 2](../../../../../../../../parts/oobb_part_holder_tool_marker_sharpie_2_place_5x5x15/assembly/render/raw/step_02.png)
3. Bring the halves together until their rims meet. Hold the capped markers vertically above their openings. [View step 3](../../../../../../../../parts/oobb_part_holder_tool_marker_sharpie_2_place_5x5x15/assembly/render/raw/step_03.png)
4. Lower the tools through the top openings. Each marker rests at its pocket floor with the cap accessible. [View step 4](../../../../../../../../parts/oobb_part_holder_tool_marker_sharpie_2_place_5x5x15/assembly/render/raw/step_04.png)
