## Assembly

Print two copies of the holder STL. The dark teal half sits against the wall and the light teal half faces the room.

1. Orient the rear half as it sits on the wall, with the pocket facing you and the opening at the top. [View step 1](../../../../../../../../parts/oobb_part_holder_tool_timer_round_80mm_1_place_7x3x34/assembly/render/raw/step_01.png)
2. Face the front half toward the rear pocket. Align the mounting holes and both top openings. [View step 2](../../../../../../../../parts/oobb_part_holder_tool_timer_round_80mm_1_place_7x3x34/assembly/render/raw/step_02.png)
3. Bring the halves together until their rims meet. Hold the round timer with its face towards the room and align it above the curved pocket. [View step 3](../../../../../../../../parts/oobb_part_holder_tool_timer_round_80mm_1_place_7x3x34/assembly/render/raw/step_03.png)
4. Lower the tool through the top opening. Lower the timer until its rim rests on the curved pocket floor. [View step 4](../../../../../../../../parts/oobb_part_holder_tool_timer_round_80mm_1_place_7x3x34/assembly/render/raw/step_04.png)
