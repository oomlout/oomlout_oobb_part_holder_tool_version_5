## Assembly

Print two copies of the holder STL. The dark teal half sits against the wall and the light teal half faces the room.

1. Orient the rear half as it sits on the wall, with the pocket facing you and the opening at the top. [View step 1](../../../../../../../../parts/oobb_part_holder_tool_spanner_m8_1_place_3x5x7d5/assembly/render/raw/step_01.png)
2. Face the front half toward the rear pocket. Align the mounting holes and both top openings. [View step 2](../../../../../../../../parts/oobb_part_holder_tool_spanner_m8_1_place_3x5x7d5/assembly/render/raw/step_02.png)
3. Bring the halves together until their rims meet. Point the open jaws down and centre them over the pocket. [View step 3](../../../../../../../../parts/oobb_part_holder_tool_spanner_m8_1_place_3x5x7d5/assembly/render/raw/step_03.png)
4. Lower the tool through the top opening. The open head rests at the pocket floor and the ring end remains accessible above the holder. [View step 4](../../../../../../../../parts/oobb_part_holder_tool_spanner_m8_1_place_3x5x7d5/assembly/render/raw/step_04.png)
