## Assembly and loading

Print two copies of the holder STL. The dark teal half sits against the wall. The light teal half faces the room. The opening and tool handles point up.

1. Orient the rear half as it sits on the wall, with the pocket facing you and the opening at the top.
2. Face the front half toward the rear pocket. Align the mounting holes and both top openings.
3. Bring the halves together until their rims meet. Hold the bit vertically above the round pocket.
4. Lower the tool through the top opening. The lower tip rests at the bottom and the upper tip remains accessible.

## Model and fit

Representative 60 mm quarter-inch double-ended PH2/PZ2 bit. Length and drive size follow the HiKOKI product. The tapered hexagonal tips are simplified and their length and end diameter are estimates. The illustrated pose was checked against the production STL at the original model scale. Physical fit remains unverified.
