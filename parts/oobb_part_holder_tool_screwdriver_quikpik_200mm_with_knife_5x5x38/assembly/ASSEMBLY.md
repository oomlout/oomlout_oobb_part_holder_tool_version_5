## Assembly and loading

Print the supplied two-half layout once. The dark teal half sits against the wall. The light teal half faces the room. The opening and tool handles point up.

1. Orient the rear half as it sits on the wall, with the pocket facing you and the opening at the top.
2. Face the front half toward the rear pocket. Align the mounting holes and both top openings.
3. Bring the halves together until their rims meet. Keep the utility knife closed and align it edge-on with its slot. Point the multi-driver bit down into the round pocket.
4. Lower the tools through the top openings. The closed knife and multi-driver sit in their separate pockets with their handles accessible.

## Model and fit

Representative Picquic SIXPAC Plus, 200 mm overall and 32 mm handle diameter from a distributor listing. Picquic specifies 3-inch quarter-inch bits and a 7/16-inch hex bolster. Exposed bit, shank and bolster lengths are estimates for the stored-bit pose. The handle contour, stored bits and workshop specimen have not been measured.
